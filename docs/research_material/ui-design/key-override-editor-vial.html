Vial's key override editor

Redrawn from vial-gui's source, not a screenshot. VIA has no key overrides.

Vial (vial-gui, a Vial protocol 6 board, 32 slots)

What it shows, and what it leaves out

  • Key Overrides is a tab of its own, after Combos; the board is not drawn. Tabs 1 to 32 across the top, numbered from 1 (the firmware counts from 0); with 32 they scroll sideways behind two arrows. Nothing marks a slot in use: every tab looks the same.
  • One slot is one grid of eight labelled rows, centred, scrolling when the window is short. Trigger and Replacement are Vial's key boxes: a click opens the keycode palette at the bottom of the window (click one here).
  • Each modifier mask is the same eight boxes, left above right: Trigger mods, Negative mods, Suppressed mods. Ticking both Shifts means "either Shift". Nothing explains the three masks.
  • Layers: always sixteen boxes, whatever the board's layer count, with Enable all / Disable all.
  • Options: six boxes, worded after QMK's source comments. The last two labels are swapped (outlined): the box saying "Don't deactivate when another key is pressed down" sets the no_reregister_trigger bit, and the other one sets no_unregister_on_other_key_down.
  • Written at once: every tick or key change writes that slot, 10 bytes, one round trip. No Save, no Revert. A Boot replacement asks for the unlock first.
  • A reset slot is all zeros (the "new slot" example): disabled, no layer ticked, no option ticked. Ticking Enable alone is not enough: with no layer it never fires, and nothing says so. No option ticked is read by QMK as all three activations.
  • Suppressed mods are not filled in from the trigger mods, as QMK's own ko_make_basic() does: forget them and Shift + Backspace sends Shift + Delete (a permanent delete in Windows Explorer).
  • No sentence says what the slot does, and nothing warns about two slots with the same trigger (the lower slot wins).
